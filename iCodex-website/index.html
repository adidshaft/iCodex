How It Works

Set it up once, then jump in whenever Codex needs you.

The flow is intentionally simple: install the Mac helper, install the iPhone app, then pair them on the same network. After that, your phone becomes the quick-control surface for your Codex machine.

01

Install iCodex-Connect

Download the Mac app from this site and launch it from your Applications folder.

It runs in the menu bar and starts the local bridge for Codex.
02

Allow permissions once

Grant Accessibility access so the Mac companion can relay actions to the Codex desktop app.

If remote controls are unavailable, click Accessibility in the iCodex-Connect menu bar app to open the right macOS settings pane.
03

Pair your phone

Open the iPhone app, scan the QR code or enter the 6-digit passcode, and connect over the same Wi-Fi.

Once paired, you can monitor threads and respond without returning to your desk.
