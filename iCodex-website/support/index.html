Support

Install, pair, and recover fast.

Updated: 2026-03-16. These setup and troubleshooting notes come from the current iCodex support docs in the repo.

Setup

  1. Download iCodex-Connect for macOS from the official website.
  2. Move the app to /Applications and open it from there.
  3. Grant Accessibility permission on your Mac when prompted.
  4. Open the Codex desktop app on your Mac.
  5. Make sure your Mac and iPhone or iPad are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  6. Pair using the 6-digit passcode shown by the Mac menu bar app.

Troubleshooting

My Mac is not found

  • Make sure iCodex-Connect is running.
  • Confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Confirm Local Network access is enabled for iCodex in iOS Settings.
  • If needed, enter your Mac’s local IP address manually.

Pairing does not work

  • Use the current 6-digit passcode from the Mac menu bar app.
  • Try disconnecting and pairing again from the iOS app settings.

Remote control actions fail

  • Verify that iCodex-Connect still has Accessibility permission.
  • Verify that the Codex desktop app is open and responsive.

The Mac app will not launch cleanly

  • Open it from /Applications, not from the mounted DMG.
  • Reinstall the latest signed and notarized version from the official website.
