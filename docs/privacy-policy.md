# iCodex Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-03-16

## Summary

iCodex is an iPhone and iPad companion app for software running on your own Mac. The iOS app connects to the iCodex-Connect companion app on your Mac so you can view threads, send replies, and manage Codex remotely.

## Information the app stores on your device

The iOS app stores the following locally on your device:
- the server host or IP address you choose
- the server port
- the pairing token used to reconnect to your Mac

This information is stored to keep the app connected to your Mac and is not uploaded to the developer by the app.

## Local network access

iCodex requests Local Network permission so it can discover and connect directly to your Mac on the same network.

## Data processing

When you use the app, requests and responses are exchanged directly between your iPhone or iPad and the iCodex-Connect companion app running on your Mac. Conversation content displayed in the app comes from your own Codex environment.

## What the app does not do

The current iOS app does not:
- include third-party advertising SDKs
- include third-party analytics SDKs
- sell your data
- track you across other companies’ apps or websites
- create a developer-hosted user account for the app

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, publish a support contact on your website before release.
