How to use it

Three concise steps and you are moving.

01

Install the Mac companion

Drop iCodex-Connect into Applications, open it, and approve Accessibility when macOS asks.

02

Pair your phone on the same Wi-Fi

Open iCodex on iPhone, enter the pairing details from your Mac, and confirm the bridge is live.

03

Run, monitor, and interrupt threads remotely

Start tasks, track token growth, search threads, and stop anything that is drifting.
