Privacy policy

Your iPhone connects to your Mac, not to a developer cloud.

Updated: 2026-03-16. iCodex is an iPhone and iPad companion app for software running on your own Mac. The iOS app connects to the iCodex-Connect companion app on your Mac so you can view threads, send replies, and manage Codex remotely.

Information stored on your device

  • the server host or IP address you choose
  • the server port
  • the pairing token used to reconnect to your Mac

This information is stored to keep the app connected to your Mac and is not uploaded to the developer by the app.

Local network access

iCodex requests Local Network permission so it can discover and connect directly to your Mac on the same network.

Data processing

Requests and responses are exchanged directly between your iPhone or iPad and the iCodex-Connect companion app running on your Mac. Conversation content displayed in the app comes from your own Codex environment.

What the app does not do

No tracking stack

  • no third-party advertising SDKs
  • no third-party analytics SDKs
  • no tracking across other apps or websites

No data sale

  • no data sale
  • no data brokerage
  • no ad network sharing

No hosted app account

The current iOS app does not create a developer-hosted user account for the app.

Questions

For setup help, use the support page. For privacy questions, publish a support contact on your website before release.
